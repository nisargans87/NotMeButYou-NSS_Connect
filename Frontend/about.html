About National Service Scheme

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a Central Sector Scheme of the Government of India. Its motto is "Not Me But You." NSS aims to develop the personality and character of students through community service.

History of NSS

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a flagship youth development programme of the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India. It was launched on 24 September 1969 to mark the 100th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi. The programme was introduced with the objective of developing students' personalities through community service while encouraging them to become responsible and socially aware citizens. The concept of NSS originated from the recommendations of the Education Commission (Kothari Commission, 1964–1966), which emphasized that students should actively participate in community service as a part of their education. Based on these recommendations, the Government of India established NSS with an initial strength of 40,000 student volunteers from 37 universities across the country. Since its establishment, NSS has grown into one of the largest voluntary student organizations in India. Today, it functions in universities, colleges, higher secondary schools, and technical institutions, involving millions of student volunteers in various social service activities. The programme serves as a bridge between educational institutions and society by encouraging students to understand community needs and contribute to solving local problems.

Objectives of NSS

1. To understand the community in which students work and identify its needs and problems.
2. To develop the personality of students through active participation in community service.
3. To promote social responsibility and encourage students to contribute towards the welfare of society.
4. To develop leadership qualities by organizing and participating in various community development programmes.
5. To encourage teamwork and cooperation while working with people from different backgrounds.
6. To foster national integration by promoting unity, harmony, and respect for India's cultural diversity.
7. To create awareness about health, hygiene, sanitation, environmental protection, and social issues.
8. To encourage voluntary service without expecting any personal reward or recognition.
9. To promote the spirit of selfless service based on the NSS motto, "Not Me But You."
10. To develop communication and interpersonal skills through interaction with local communities and public organizations.
11. To encourage environmental conservation through tree plantation, cleanliness drives, waste management, and water conservation activities.
12. To develop disaster preparedness by training volunteers to assist during natural disasters and emergencies.
13. To improve problem-solving abilities by involving students in identifying and addressing community challenges.
14. To promote health awareness through blood donation camps, health check-up camps, vaccination drives, and awareness programmes.
15. To build responsible citizens who actively participate in nation-building and contribute to the overall development of society.
